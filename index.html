A smarter day starts with five squares.

MiniCross gives you quick 5x5 crosswords that actually work with your schedule. Solve one during your morning commute, over coffee, or before bed. Each puzzle's pretty quick but still makes you think — just a few minutes to get your brain going without eating up your whole day.

Small grid. Big mental boost.

We kept MiniCross super simple on purpose. We make a new 5x5 crossword every day that's easy to start but still makes you think. Sure, they're tiny puzzles, but we don't half-ass the clues — each one's meant to make you go 'hmm' for a second. It’s the kind of daily habit that feels productive without feeling demanding.

A fresh crossword every single day.

Consistency builds habit. Habit builds skill. MiniCross delivers a new puzzle at the same time every day, designed to keep you coming back without demanding hours of commitment.

  • Designed for completion in minutes

  • Balanced difficulty

  • No long sessions

  • Daily return loop

Designed for focus.

  • Fresh daily mini crosswords

  • No signups or unnecessary distractions

  • Offline play after initial puzzle load

  • 5x5 grid format for quick solving

  • Clean, minimal interface

  • Smooth, responsive controls

It's clean and simple, so you can just focus on solving without any distractions getting in the way.

Train memory. Strengthen vocabulary. Stay sharp.

When you're doing crosswords, your brain's basically juggling like five different things at once. You recall vocabulary, interpret clues, and connect letters logically across the grid. Playing regularly might help with your focus and vocabulary — it's like a little workout for your brain. A few minutes a day can make a noticeable difference over time.
